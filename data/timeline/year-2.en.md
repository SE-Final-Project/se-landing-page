## Year 2: SE Process & Web Application Development

Departmental activities designed to strengthen bonds and networks between seniors and juniors. This year introduces **"Module 2: Software Engineering Process and Web Application Development"**, where you begin building tangible software products and learning how to manage your own engineering workflow.

### Yearly Learning Outcomes
- **Process:** Understand and follow the **Individual Software Process** — personal time management, production planning, and finding and fixing defects in your own code.
- **Analysis & Design:** Gather requirements, perform object-oriented analysis and design, design user interfaces (UI) and databases, and interpret UML diagrams.
- **Software Development:** Build small, clean software applications using open-source tools and appropriate programming languages.
- **Ethics:** Appreciate and respect others' intellectual work through proper citation and avoiding plagiarism.

### Highlight Experience
You will attend the **"Software Development Training Camp"**, an intensive off-campus overnight camp where you collaborate with a team for at least 48 working hours to build a web application and earn a Web Application Development certificate.
