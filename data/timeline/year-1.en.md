## Year 1: Adaptation & Software Engineering Foundation

Orientation atmosphere welcoming new students into university life. In the first year, the curriculum focuses on **"Module 1: Software Engineering Foundation"**, applying computational fundamentals and programming principles through project-based learning.

### Yearly Learning Outcomes
- **Attitude & Adaptation:** Adapt effectively to university-level study, take responsibility for individual and team work, follow university regulations, and value punctuality.
- **Logic & Mathematics:** Define and explain core concepts in discrete mathematics, basic statistics, and systematic, logical thinking.
- **Programming:** Analyze problems, design program workflows, and write foundational programs (both structured and Object-Oriented Programming - OOP) to solve straightforward problems.
- **Tools:** Use essential software development tools and search effectively for software engineering references online.

### What You Will Experience
You will join our signature student mentorship system called **"Makul (Cluster)"** to practice teamwork from Day 1, build presentation and communication skills, and participate in creative ice-breaking activities.
