## Year 4: Professionalism, Seminar and Project

Fourth-year students prepare to step into the professional world as software engineers. The final year brings full industry immersion under **"Module 4: Software Engineering Seminar and Project"**, developing digital talent for the Eastern Economic Corridor (EEC) and the nation.

### Yearly Learning Outcomes
- **Semester 1 – Cooperative Education (CWIE):** Work full-time on-site at a partner organization for at least 16 weeks (or 600 working hours), applying everything you have learned to solve real industry problems and adapting to corporate culture.
- **Semester 2 – Senior Software Engineering Project:** Conduct independent research and deliver a complete capstone project end-to-end (requirements gathering -> design -> implementation -> testing -> delivery) addressing real industry needs.
- **Professionalism:** Demonstrate high accountability and consistent integrity, research international academic literature, and present software engineering work professionally to stakeholders.

### Graduation Outlook
You will graduate not merely as a coder, but as a **"Software Engineer"** who understands business context, project management, teamwork, and delivering production-grade software.
