## Year 3: Medium to Large Scale Software Development

The OSSD (Open Source Software Developers) project builds collaborative software engineering experience. This is the most intensive academic year under **"Module 3: Medium to Large Scale Software Development"**, simulating real enterprise workflows, cross-course integration, and collaboration with industry partners.

### Yearly Learning Outcomes
- **Enterprise Teamwork:** Work effectively in teams as both a leader and a team member, resolve team conflicts, prioritize tasks, and handle unexpected challenges.
- **Enterprise Software Development:** Plan and estimate projects, gather requirements, design software architectures, develop, and perform Quality Assurance (QA) testing for medium-to-large, complex software systems.
- **Collaborative Process:** Apply Agile and workgroup software development processes in realistic industry-like environments.
- **Quality & Open Source:** Ensure software meets quality standards through rigorous testing while applying reuse, extensibility, and open-source sharing principles.

### Highlight Experience
Courses use real-world problem statements from partner companies. Your team's deliverables are evaluated iteratively in sprints by both faculty members and industry representatives.
